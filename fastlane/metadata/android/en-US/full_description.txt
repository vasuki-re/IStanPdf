A foss offline app for PDF & DOCX operations.

## Features -

- **Merge PDF** - Combine multiple PDF files into one.
- **Modify PDF** - Remove or reorder pages within a PDF.
- **Compress PDF** - Reduce file size by resolution or custom target size.

- **Image to PDF** - Convert images into a single PDF document.
- **PDF to Image** - Extract PDF pages into a ZIP file of images.
- **DOCX to PDF** - Convert Word documents into PDF files.
- **MD to PDF** - Convert Markdown or plain text files into PDFs.

- **Remove Pages** - Delete specific pages from DOCX files.
- **Reorder Pages** - Rearrange pages within DOCX files.
